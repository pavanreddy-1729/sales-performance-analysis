# 📊 Sales Performance and Revenue Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data to uncover revenue trends, top-performing products, and regional performance.
The analysis is performed using **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to generate insights that can help businesses make better data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall **sales performance**
* Identify **top-selling products**
* Evaluate **regional sales distribution**
* Track **revenue trends over time**
* Build an **interactive Power BI dashboard**

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Python** (Pandas, Matplotlib)
* **SQL** (Data querying and aggregation)
* **Power BI** (Data visualization and dashboard)
* **Git & GitHub**
* **VS Code**

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 1️⃣ Sales Trend Analysis

* Monthly and regional sales trends
* Identification of revenue growth patterns

### 2️⃣ Product Performance

* Determining top-selling products
* Identifying low-performing items

### 3️⃣ Regional Analysis

* Comparing revenue across regions
* Understanding geographic sales distribution

### 4️⃣ Category Analysis

* Evaluating revenue contribution from different product categories

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* Some regions contribute significantly more revenue than others.
* A small number of products generate the majority of sales.
* Electronics category drives most of the total revenue.
